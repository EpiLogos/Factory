# Develop a verifiable change

Read the relevant owner ground and implementation before choosing the form. State observable acceptance at the commission's grain. Record materially different alternatives and the reason for the chosen form when a real design choice exists. Do not inflate a mechanical change into a design exercise.

Identify blocking prerequisites, independent work and shared mutable subjects. Select units that leave an inspectable state. Verify a dependent unit before building further on its result; independent branches may progress in parallel and converge through the native proof obligations. A migration's intermediate check does not certify its final whole.

Use the affected product's domain and public boundary. When repeated mechanical work justifies a deterministic lever, prove the lever on actual inputs and retain the per-unit discriminator. Do not automate uncertainty into repeated edits.

Verify the required interaction and data path against the changed artifact, including materially affected consumers. Check whether an existing source or documentation relation became stale and return that discrepancy to its owner. Ordered commits can help review when requested or locally warranted; automatic trunk rebases and mandatory PR stacks are not part of this Method.

Return the chosen form, resulting behavior, evidence and open obligations. A missing required capability remains in the Whole even when the completed portion is polished.
