# Factory development and self-improvement

Use this Skill when developmental pressure should become a bounded Factory Run, including proposals to improve a native product or its authoritative Skill source.

## Development law

Factory coordinates development; it does not acquire arbitrary authority over the Project or product it is changing. Recover what the Project is before locally convenient feature decomposition becomes architectural authority. Locate native owners and consume their authoritative public contracts. Generated/projected artifacts and installed runtime state are not the normal authoring source.

For product-meaning work, orient through the smallest sufficient chain actually present:

```text
human-authored Ground / intended experience
  -> SemanticWiki / Project vocabulary
  -> StructuralGround where applicable
  -> semantic <-> local-description <-> exact-code anchors
  -> Project praxis / capability condition
  -> current frontier
```

ProjectCentral, SemanticWiki, ProjectMap, CodeIndex, Skill/Method resolution and Actuation Agency remain owned by their native systems. Factory retains attributable refs and evidence; it does not recreate their stores or resolvers.

## Proof over plausibility

A developmental `Claim` is an attributable proposed determination: what an Agent presently considers plausible about a deficiency, change, result or outcome. That is exactly why a Claim must not be allowed to certify itself. Plausibility, confidence, coherence, visual resemblance to the requested result, a successful local check or a persuasive narrative are not substitutes for the metrics by which the whole requested outcome is actually verified.

Before work that can end in a consequential completion Claim, recover the **operative Whole** at the grain named by the Project/Intent/destination and make its materially relevant closure conditions explicit. Use the current Factory verification semantics rather than a parallel task format:

```text
operative Whole / authored or recognised desired condition
    -> Claim(s) that operationalise what may be true
    -> VerificationRequirement / effective VerificationPlan
    -> proof-bearing obligations in RunMap / Gate relations as appropriate
    -> exact Check / Evidence / Assessment / Review
    -> Closure determination
    -> Gate / Recognition / Return
```

The ledger principle is simple: every material obligation which bears on the completion Claim must have an explicit standing. `satisfied`, `contradicted`, `insufficient-evidence`, `blocked`, `inapplicable` or another owner-defined terminal state may all be honest results; an omitted obligation is not a passing obligation. The Agent may discover or revise the map as reality is learned, but it may not silently shrink the Whole until the work it happened to complete looks sufficient.

`representative evidence` is evidence for what it actually represents. It establishes a whole only when the operative verification requirement says that sampling is sufficient and the coverage/sampling condition is itself demonstrated. A screenshot, rendered artifact, green subset of tests, plausible implementation path or Agent self-report must never be promoted into evidence for untested dimensions merely because the overall result looks right.

Where the verification field discloses genuinely independent obligations, execute or assess them in parallel where suitable and converge through explicit barriers. When a verifier can deterministically rerun a proving command or inspect a source of truth, prefer that over trusting the producing Agent's statement that the command passed. Where the plan requires independent Assessment, preserve independent Execution/AgentSession lineage.

Factory already owns the durable `RunThoughtField` and its cognitive read optic, while Central owns the landed `NOW` / `DAY` temporal working field. These are not extra planning stores. They are source-backed current material which can participate in the same whole-relative orientation through the existing AIKit Knowledge Navigation / SemanticWiki / Contemplate seam.

Where materially useful, consume a bounded reading equivalent to:

```text
Central NOW / DAY current refs

        ↓ AIKit whole disclosure
```

The current QL shape repertoire may structure that aggregate at whatever grain the material supports — small positive wholes through progressively conjugated/twelvefold forms, D1–D3, `4×4`, `6×6` and relational-generation forms. Factory does not copy those shapes into `RunThoughtField`, convert NOW items into QL buckets, or turn every disclosed address into Run topology. The shaped reading is attributable developmental cognition: it can expose a missing obligation, dependency, independence, contradiction or new Claim, which then enters Factory through the existing authorised Claim/RunMap/Evidence semantics.

This is also the correct continuity for later Vāk/Ta-Onta integration. Vāk can eventually address the same disclosed whole/positions/relations; Anima-like orchestration can traverse selected Run determinations; Aletheia/Recognition-like return can reconcile evidence against the whole. No future module identity is required for the current generic Run/Thought/NOW relation.

## Orientation Methodologies

A developmental undertaking is oriented by two Methodologies that compose in parallel, neither above the other. Factory consumes both from their owners and restates neither:

```text
Wayfinder                     skill/personal/wayfinder (METHODOLOGY:)
    developmental topology    destination, scope, dependencies, frontier,
                              ownership, evidence, closure, map revision
Documentation Methodology     skill/central/docs-methodology (METHODOLOGY:)
    representation topology   Vision, Design, Mockup, Architecture, diagrams,
                              capability relations, source reconciliation
```

The RunMap is the Wayfinder map: a Decision node is a ticket, the RunMap frontier is the Wayfinder frontier, and `JourneyReturn.wayfinder_refs` carries the map relation back. For a UI or architecture commission, Wayfinder names the undertaking and its frontier; the Documentation Methodology names the source layer the work pressures; knowledge navigation and Jev narrow the context; a Method (a documentation Method such as `skill/central/ui-development`, or `factory-bounded-work`) carries the work; verification returns evidence; the Return revises the map and/or the documentation field through their owners.

An Agent carries these as SkillSets (`central:core-development`, `central:documentation`); membership is repertoire, not a loaded body. A small local repair selects neither Methodology and loads no Vision.

## Procedure

1. **Receive pressure as evidence, not command truth.** Record the observation/request and its provenance. A relation-field observation, benchmark result or repeated use may open investigation; none automatically creates a backlog item or mutation.
2. **Recover Project meaning, current cognition and ownership.** Confirm Project / Focus. Where product meaning matters, read human-authored Ground before deep architecture work. Traverse maintained semantic knowledge and applicable local structural source before treating code-local decomposition as design authority. Include relevant current NOW/DAY and RunThought material only when it bears on the commission, through their existing owner/provider refs rather than transcript replay. Identify the repository/product that owns the concept, Action or Skill source.
3. **Retain exact reflection anchors.** Where AIKit/ProjectMap reflection is available, retain only the semantic ref, attributable local source ref, exact CodeReference(s), verification refs and provider revision materially used. A local description is source/contract material, not implementation truth; derived code topology is implementation intelligence, not authored meaning.
4. **Resolve praxis and whole disclosure through their owners.** Use AIKit-owned Method/Skill/SkillSet/Profile/UsageOverlay/Context resolution where present. When the task needs relational-whole reasoning, consume AIKit's bounded WikiFrame/Contemplate reading over the relevant Wiki/Flow/NOW/RunThought sources and preserve its exact basis/shape/provenance refs. Record the smallest sufficient resolved condition for a consequential Run; do not parse generated harness state into a Factory-local Method, Skill, QL or knowledge store.
5. **Form the developmental Claim as a proposal, not completion.** State what appears deficient, desired or uncertain; identify the operative Whole/Intent/destination against which the Claim matters; preserve the source wording where an authored condition exists; record the Agent's operationalisation separately; and state what evidence would support, contradict or leave the Claim unresolved. Confidence does not change its standing.
6. **Build the whole-relative verification ledger and open the bounded Run.** Give the Run a destination, RunMap frontier, expected owner seam and explicit verification/Recognition criteria. Project materially relevant completion obligations into Work/Gate/Requires/ConvergesTo/ReturnsTo structure where the current RunMap contract supports it. Preserve independent branches as independent rather than serialising them for convenience. Do not silently remove an unmet condition merely to obtain Closure.
7. **Develop a Candidate through the owner's public seam.** Use owner SDK/domain/application contracts. Do not teach direct mutation of generated projections, installed caches or private foreign state as the default path.
8. **Discharge verification obligations.** Run or request the owner-native checks, conformance paths, scenario proofs, inspections, Assessments and Reviews required by the effective verification plan. Bind resulting Evidence to the exact Claim/subject state and obligation it actually proves. A passing subset remains a passing subset. Representative/sampled evidence may support whole Closure only where its sufficiency and coverage are explicit in the verification requirement. Retain negative, adversarial, missing and contradictory evidence rather than smoothing it into an overall success impression.
9. **Check returned intelligibility.** After meaningful structural change, ask whether an applicable local description, governance source, ProjectMap relation, Wiki account, RunThought/NOW account or verification account became stale. Do not force every edit to rewrite documentation; do not leave an existing durable articulation silently less intelligible.
10. **Return the attributable difference and verification state.** Present Candidate, Claims, Evidence, trade-offs, reflection/praxis discrepancy, owner-source diff/revision and the whole-relative verification ledger. State exactly which obligations are satisfied, contradicted, blocked, inapplicable or insufficiently evidenced and whether Closure is actually warranted. Return meaningful cognitive/temporal differences through the existing RunThought/NOW/Wiki owner seams where appropriate; do not replace this with an Agent-authored “done” summary.
11. **Promote explicitly or return.** Wiki/source/Ground/Skill/Method pressure returns as a proposal to the native owner and, where required, human Recognition. Successful execution, model scoring, repeated use and benchmark wins are fitness evidence only. A successful Run, plausible Candidate or high-confidence Claim does not itself establish Closure or promotion.

## Native Skill revision procedure

For Skill development, preserve the full chain:

```text
native Skill source + exact revision
  -> observed deficiency / request
  -> Factory Claim + bounded Run
  -> proposed owner-source Skill revision
  -> explicit verification obligations
  -> Skill validation + representative use/evidence at their actual scope
  -> whole-relative Closure determination
  -> native-owner review / Recognition
  -> explicit promotion, rollback point retained
```

Praxis configuration is an input condition. Praxis fitness is returned evidence. Never treat an edited harness projection as canonical source. Never let `SkillSet` selection imply Root position or metagency. Never convert successful use into automatic trust.

## Structural fidelity

If the target has constitutive structural source, compose with `StructuralGround` rather than assuming name-level parity. Distinguish source, identity/coordinate, relation, operational and experiential fidelity. If a declared source/code relation is stale, record the discrepancy and route a proposal to the source owner; do not silently rewrite the source or flatten a target-owned coordinate into generic Factory ontology.

## Prepared NOW and Jev-assisted development

When AIKit supplies a participant-specific prepared NOW view for a commissioned Run, treat it as an operative reading of existing owners, not a Factory-owned memory record. Preserve the Run/WorkflowUnit revisions, participant/dependency relations, exact Central SourceRefs/revisions, Wiki routes, preparation basis and the delivered prepared version. The same undertaking may legitimately prepare different worker, related-worker and verifier views; verifier expectations and sibling-private material are not developmental context for another participant.

Use exact Factory task/dependency/Agency/Return relations first. Jev may classify additional semantic relevance across documented capabilities or decide whether a result should remain task-local, inform Wiki/practice, or pressure a matrix/account relation. That does not grant a write, recognise a Candidate, close a Run or replace the Factory verification ledger.

After a consequential Return, publish the native Factory mutation/evidence first. Then let affected participant contexts receive the returned ref/revision through their normal continuation boundary. Reconnect/restart must de-duplicate the same Return identity and preserve uncertain prior effects rather than manufacturing a second completed contribution. Warm prepared-context reuse is valid only while its source/dependency/disclosure basis remains current.
